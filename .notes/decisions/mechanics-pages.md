# Mechanics page list

Status: decided, 2026-09-19

## Context

The spec left open whether React, Vite, Tailwind, and shadcn get Mechanics
pages beside primitives, icons, charts, fonts, and animation.

## Options

- Keep only the five pages that already exist.
- Add React, Vite, Tailwind, and shadcn as their own pages.

## Decision

Yes. The Mechanics section is nine pages: Primitives, Icons, Charts, Fonts,
Animation, React, Vite, Tailwind, and shadcn. They document what Sand is built
on, not a swap catalogue.

## Consequences

- Ticket 16 writes the four new pages and brings every Mechanics page up to
  the agreed format (showcase, parts, links, alternatives).
- The spec TODO for this list is closed.
