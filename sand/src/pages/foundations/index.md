# foundations

Foundation pages. Tokens in the preview container, then a table of roles or the
scale, then the rules.

- [colors.tsx](colors.tsx) - semantic color tokens, pairing, chart and sidebar
- [typography.tsx](typography.tsx) - text roles
- [fonts.tsx](fonts.tsx) - the three faces and where each is legal
- [spacing.tsx](spacing.tsx) - named steps over the numeric scale
- [radius.tsx](radius.tsx) - four radius steps around the base
- [shadow.tsx](shadow.tsx) - inherited lift steps in light and dark
- [motion.tsx](motion.tsx) - duration and easing, enter and exit, reduced motion
- [icons.tsx](icons.tsx) - icons in use, sizes, and text pairing
